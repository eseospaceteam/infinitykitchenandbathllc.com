Who We Are

More Than a Contractor — A Partner in Your Home

Infinity Kitchens and Baths was founded in 2011 with a simple belief: homeowners deserve a remodeling contractor they can trust — one who listens, delivers what they promise, and stands behind their work long after the job is done.

Our founder brings over 35 years of hands-on remodeling experience to every project. That depth of knowledge means we catch problems before they happen, recommend solutions that actually work, and produce results that last for decades.

We're a small, family-owned business — and that's intentional. It means every project gets personal attention. You'll deal directly with the people who care most about getting it right.

On-Time, Every Time

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Infinity Kitchens and Baths licensed and insured?

Yes. We are a licensed Arizona contractor (AZ ROC #339999), bonded, and fully insured — with liability and workers' compensation coverage on every job. You can verify our license anytime through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

What areas does Infinity Kitchens and Baths serve?

We serve Prescott, Prescott Valley, Chino Valley, Dewey-Humboldt, Sedona, Cottonwood, Camp Verde, Mayer, Cordes Lakes, Williamson Valley, and the greater Yavapai County area. Call 928-800-1998 to confirm coverage for your address.

What makes Infinity different from other remodelers?

Three things: factory-direct pricing (we source materials straight from manufacturers, so you skip retail markups), a background-checked and drug-tested crew you can trust in your home, and more than 35 years of combined experience backed by honest, fixed quotes — no surprise charges.

Does Infinity offer free design consultations?

Yes. Every project starts with a free in-home consultation where we bring computer-aided design tools and material samples, discuss your goals, and provide a detailed, no-obligation quote.
