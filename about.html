Who We Are

More Than a Contractor — A Partner in Your Home

Infinity Kitchen and Bath was founded in 2013 with a simple belief: homeowners deserve a remodeling contractor they can trust — one who listens, delivers what they promise, and stands behind their work long after the job is done.

Our founder brings over 35 years of hands-on remodeling experience to every project. That depth of knowledge means we catch problems before they happen, recommend solutions that actually work, and produce results that last for decades.

We're a small, family-owned business — and that's intentional. It means every project gets personal attention. You'll deal directly with the people who care most about getting it right.

What Sets Us Apart

Our Core Values

Factory Direct Pricing

We source materials directly from manufacturers so you get premium products — quartz, granite, flooring, fixtures — at prices that can't be matched at retail.

Honest & Fair Pricing

We encourage you to get multiple bids. We price our work fairly and stand by every quote. No surprise charges, no bait-and-switch — what we quote is what you pay.

Vetted, Trusted Team

Every member of our team passes a background check and drug testing. You can feel comfortable with our crew in your home — that's a promise we take seriously.

On-Time, Every Time

Your time matters. We show up when we say we will, communicate proactively, and complete projects on the schedule we've agreed to — no excuses.

Free Design Consultation

We come to you with computer-aided design tools, material samples, and real ideas. Our in-home consultations are completely free — no pressure, no obligation.

Fully Insured

We carry full liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every job. You're protected, your property is protected, and our team is protected.
