How It Works

Our Bathroom Remodeling Process, Start to Finish

Every Infinity Kitchen and Bath project follows a proven six-step process designed to keep you informed, on schedule, and fully satisfied at every stage.

01

Free In-Home Consultation & Design

Your bathroom remodel begins with a free, no-obligation consultation at your home. One of our designers visits your space, takes detailed measurements, and has an honest conversation about your goals — what you want to accomplish, what's frustrating you about the current bathroom, and what features matter most. This isn't a sales call. It's a design conversation, and there's never any pressure to sign on the same day.

From that discussion, our design team creates a computer-aided 3D rendering of your new bathroom. You'll see the tile choices, vanity placement, shower layout, and fixture selections rendered in detail before we touch a single wall. This step eliminates surprises, prevents costly mid-project changes, and gives you the confidence to move forward knowing exactly what you're getting. Most consultations take 60–90 minutes and are completely free of charge.

02

Material Selection & Factory-Direct Ordering

Once the design is approved, we move into material selection. Because Infinity Kitchen and Bath purchases materials factory-direct, you'll have access to tile, shower systems, vanities, countertops, fixtures, and flooring at prices that are often significantly below what you'd find at retail home improvement stores. This isn't a standard trade discount — it's a structural advantage we've built into our business model since we opened in 2013.

Our team guides you through every selection with real-world context: which tile choices hold up to daily use, which shower systems are easiest to maintain, which countertop surfaces make sense for your bathroom's traffic level and cleaning habits. Every material is ordered, verified, and staged before your project starts — no mid-project delays waiting on backorders that push your timeline back weeks.

03

Demolition & Subfloor Preparation

Professional demolition is more careful than it looks. Our crew protects your home throughout the demo phase — laying floor protection through all traffic areas, sealing doorways to contain dust, and carefully disconnecting plumbing and electrical before removing fixtures. We handle haul-away of all demolition debris as part of the project scope.

For older Prescott homes, particularly those built in the 1970s through 1990s, we always inspect behind walls and under floors for moisture damage, dry rot, or mold before proceeding. If we find a problem, we address it immediately and communicate the findings to you transparently. Our policy is zero surprises buried in a change order later — any additional scope gets discussed and approved before work continues. Rough plumbing and electrical work, including any repositioning of supply lines, drain lines, or circuits required by the new layout, is handled at this stage.

04

Waterproofing & Substrate Installation

The most important step in any bathroom remodel is one most homeowners never see: the waterproofing. It's what separates a bathroom that lasts 30 years from one that develops mold problems within five. We use industry-standard waterproofing membranes on all shower pans, shower walls, and wet areas — and we slope every shower floor correctly toward the drain at the required 1/4-inch-per-foot pitch.

For tile installations, we use cement backer board as the substrate on all wet surfaces, properly fastened and taped at every seam. For groutless solid-surface shower systems, the waterproofing is built directly into the panel system itself — which is one of the key reasons those systems carry a lifetime warranty. We test all waterproofing and drain connections before applying any finish surface. This step adds time to the project, but skipping it is how contractors create problems that homeowners discover years later when it's much more expensive to fix.

05

Tile, Surfaces & Fixture Installation

This is the stage where your new bathroom comes to life. Every tile installation begins with a detailed layout drawing that accounts for the tile pattern, grout joint spacing, cut placement, and edge treatments — nothing is eyeballed. Our tile setters are experienced with everything from large-format 24×48 porcelain slabs to intricate mosaic patterns and natural stone that requires sealing before grouting.

Vanities are leveled, secured to wall studs, and fitted with countertops cut to exact dimensions. Plumbing fixtures — faucets, showerheads, valves, and drain trim — are installed to manufacturer specifications and pressure-tested before walls are closed. Frameless glass shower enclosures are measured on-site and installed with proper tolerances and hardware. Lighting, exhaust ventilation, and accessories are handled as part of the finish phase, so everything is coordinated and complete before we call the project done.

06

Final Walkthrough, Punch List & Warranty

Before we consider a project complete, we conduct a thorough walkthrough with you present. We examine every tile edge, every caulk joint, every fixture connection, every door and drawer, every threshold transition. Any item that doesn't meet our standards — or yours — goes on a punch list and gets addressed before we collect final payment. This walkthrough typically takes 30–45 minutes, and our team takes it seriously.

Once complete, we review the care and maintenance guidelines for your specific materials: which cleaners to use on your tile, how to care for natural stone, how to maintain grout long-term, and what to do if you ever need warranty service. Our workmanship is warranted in writing, and many of our products carry manufacturer warranties ranging from several years to full lifetime coverage. We want you to be a customer for life — and the only way that happens is if we hold ourselves to this standard on every project, every time.

Common Questions

Bathroom Remodeling FAQs

How long does a bathroom remodel take in Prescott, AZ?

Most bathroom remodels in the Prescott area take between 2 and 5 weeks from demolition to completion. A simple tub-to-shower conversion or vanity update can often be completed in 5–7 business days. A full gut-and-rebuild of a master bathroom — custom tile shower, frameless glass, double vanity, new flooring — typically runs 3–5 weeks. Lead times vary based on material availability and overall project complexity. We provide a detailed written timeline at your consultation and give you regular progress updates so you're never left wondering where things stand.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Prescott?

It depends on the scope of work, but you don't have to worry about it. Infinity Kitchen and Bath handles all required permits and inspections for your project.

What is a groutless shower system, and is it worth the extra cost?

A groutless shower system uses solid-surface acrylic or composite panels — instead of tile — to create the shower walls and sometimes the floor. Because the surface is completely non-porous and seamless, mold and mildew can't penetrate it, and there's no grout to clean, reseal, or replace over time. These systems typically cost somewhat more to install than a standard tile shower, but the maintenance savings are substantial. No annual grout sealing, no mold remediation, no tile replacement. We back our groutless installations with a full lifetime warranty from both Infinity Kitchen and Bath and the manufacturer. The majority of customers who choose groutless say it's the best decision they made in the remodel.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Prescott, AZ?

Costs vary significantly based on scope, material selections, and whether plumbing or electrical needs to be relocated. In general, a partial update — new shower walls, new vanity, new flooring — typically runs $8,000–$15,000. A full master bathroom remodel with a custom tile shower, frameless glass enclosure, double vanity, and new flooring typically runs $18,000–$35,000 or more depending on material choices. We provide detailed written estimates after your free in-home consultation — no surprises, no pressure. We also offer financing options for qualifying projects, which allows many homeowners to do the full remodel they want rather than making compromises on scope or quality.

Can you remodel a small bathroom or half-bath in Prescott?

Absolutely — small bathrooms are actually where smart design makes the biggest difference. Strategic layout adjustments, light-colored tile, frameless glass shower enclosures, floating vanities, and well-planned storage solutions can transform a cramped bathroom into a space that feels significantly larger and more functional than its square footage would suggest. We've renovated everything from tiny guest bathrooms to narrow half-baths throughout Prescott, Prescott Valley, Chino Valley, and the surrounding area. During your free consultation, we'll bring specific design ideas tailored to your exact space and goals.

Are you licensed and insured for bathroom remodeling in Arizona?

Yes. Infinity Kitchen and Bath holds Arizona ROC License #339999 and is fully licensed, bonded, and insured for all remodeling work throughout the state. Every crew member is background-checked and drug-tested. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every job site. You should always verify contractor licensing before hiring anyone in Arizona — you can check any contractor's current status at the Arizona Registrar of Contractors website at roc.az.gov. Never hire a contractor who can't provide you with a current, active ROC license number before work begins.
