Cookie Policy

Effective Date: June 23, 2026 — Infinity Kitchens and Baths, LLC

This Cookie Policy explains how Infinity Kitchens and Baths, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on infinitykitchenandbathllc.com (the “Site”). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owner about how the site is used. “Similar technologies” include browser storage such as localStorage, which we use to remember your cookie choice.

How We Use Cookies

We keep our use of cookies deliberately minimal. We use them to remember your consent preference, to understand in aggregate how visitors find and use our Site, and to measure the performance of our Google Ads campaigns — for example, to know which ad led to a contact form submission. We do not use social media tracking cookies, and we do not sell your personal information. You can decline advertising and analytics cookies at any time using the “Cookie Preferences” link in our footer.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Cookie / Key Type Purpose Duration
ikb_consent Essential (localStorage) Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies so we don’t ask again. Until cleared

Analytics cookies are set by Google Analytics and are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. Exact cookie names and durations may change as Google updates its products.

Your Consent Choices

We use Google Consent Mode and a consent banner to manage analytics cookies based on where you are located:

  • European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland: analytics cookies are off by default and are only set after you choose “Accept” (opt-in).
  • United States and elsewhere: analytics cookies are enabled by default, and you may turn them off at any time by choosing “Decline” (opt-out).

Essential storage (your consent preference) is always used because the Site cannot remember your choice without it.

How to Manage or Withdraw Consent

  • On this Site: select Cookie Preferences (also available in the footer of every page) to reopen the banner and change your choice at any time.
  • In your browser: most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some parts of the Site function.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: you can install Google’s browser opt-out add-on to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics on any website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law, or how we operate. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
