Manufactured Home Specialists

Manufactured Home Remodeling in Cordes Lakes

We Know How Manufactured Homes Are Built

A significant portion of homes in Cordes Lakes are manufactured homes, and that matters when it comes to remodeling. Manufactured homes are built to HUD code standards rather than the IRC standards that govern site-built construction. That means different wall construction — often thinner, with different stud spacing — different plumbing access points, different subfloor systems, and weight distribution considerations that affect how cabinetry and tile can be installed.

Contractors who only work on site-built homes sometimes run into problems when they take on a manufactured home project: walls that don’t hold anchors the way they expect, plumbing that’s routed differently than anticipated, or subfloors that need reinforcement before new flooring can go down. At Infinity Kitchens and Baths, we have hands-on experience remodeling manufactured homes throughout Yavapai County. We assess the existing structure before we quote the job, we explain what we find, and we build solutions that work with how the home is actually constructed — not how we assume it must be.

Whether you want to update the kitchen cabinets in a 1990s double-wide, replace the bathroom flooring, or convert the tub to a walk-in shower, we can handle it correctly from the start.
