Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling in the Verde Valley's Fastest-Growing City

Cottonwood, AZ has developed a dual identity that sets it apart from every other community in the Verde Valley. On one side, long-time Cottonwood residents — many of whom have owned their homes for decades — are investing in kitchen and bathroom renovations to stay comfortable and current in the places they love. On the other, an influx of investors and second-home buyers drawn by the region's booming wine country tourism economy are snapping up properties in and around Old Town Cottonwood and converting them into high-performing short-term rentals. Both groups have real remodeling needs, and Infinity Kitchen and Bath serves them both.

The remodeling opportunity in Cottonwood is particularly compelling right now. Historic homes in Old Town — many built in the 1940s through 1960s — are receiving top-to-bottom kitchen and bathroom overhauls to become showpiece Airbnb and VRBO rentals that command premium nightly rates from wine-touring visitors. These projects demand materials that photograph beautifully, hold up under continuous rental occupancy, and are easy to clean and maintain between guests. At the same time, the newer neighborhoods west of town — residential subdivisions developed in the 1970s through the 1990s — are seeing a wave of traditional remodels as primary homeowners update kitchens and bathrooms that are long overdue for a refresh.

Infinity Kitchen and Bath serves Cottonwood from our Prescott base, approximately 40 miles away via SR-89A through Jerome. We do not charge a travel fee for Cottonwood projects. Our team arrives on time, works on your schedule, and brings the same licensed, insured craftsmanship — AZ ROC #339999 — that has made us the trusted remodeling contractor throughout Yavapai County. Whether your property is a historic Old Town bungalow being repositioned for wine-tourism guests or a West Cottonwood split-level that just needs a modern kitchen, we can help.

Built for Cottonwood's Market

Remodeling for Cottonwood's Wine Country Market

Short-Term Rental Remodels

Cottonwood's proximity to more than two dozen Verde Valley wineries — including Alcantara Vineyard, Javelina Leap, and Page Springs Cellars — has turned the city into a weekend destination with a booming Airbnb and VRBO market. Investors who own properties near Old Town Cottonwood need kitchens and bathrooms that photograph well for listing platforms, withstand the wear of back-to-back guest stays, and can be cleaned and reset efficiently between bookings.

Infinity builds rental-grade kitchens and bathrooms with that reality in mind. We recommend quartz countertops over marble or granite because they resist staining without periodic sealing — a real advantage in a rental environment. LVP flooring handles spills, scuffs, and rolling luggage without showing wear. Groutless shower systems eliminate the maintenance headache of discolored grout lines and make bathroom turnovers significantly faster. Every material we specify is chosen for durability and visual appeal, so your Cottonwood rental continues to earn five-star reviews and strong nightly rates year after year.

Traditional Homeowner Remodels

Not every Cottonwood project is an investment property. Long-time residents who have lived in their homes for 20, 30, or 40 years are also ready to invest in spaces that have served them well but are well past their prime. We see this particularly in West Cottonwood neighborhoods — subdivisions developed between the 1970s and 1990s where original kitchens still have laminate countertops, raised-panel oak cabinets, and outdated appliance configurations that simply no longer work for modern living.

Retirement-age homeowners make up a significant share of our Cottonwood client base. Many are looking for bathroom remodels that address mobility and safety alongside aesthetics — walk-in showers that eliminate the high step of a tub surround, curbless designs that reduce fall risk, and grab bar placements that integrate cleanly with the overall design. We offer aging-in-place solutions as part of every bathroom consultation, because for many Cottonwood families, the goal of the remodel is to stay in a home they love for another decade or two.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions — Cottonwood Remodeling

Does Infinity do remodels for short-term rental properties in Cottonwood?
Yes — short-term rental remodels are a significant part of our Cottonwood project mix. We understand the specific demands of Airbnb and VRBO properties: materials need to look great in listing photos, hold up under continuous occupancy, and be easy to clean between guests. We recommend quartz countertops, LVP flooring, and groutless shower systems for most rental projects because they combine visual appeal with low maintenance and long service life. We can assess your specific property and recommend a scope and material set that maximizes your rental's earning potential.
What permits are required for a kitchen remodel in Cottonwood?
Permit requirements in Cottonwood are governed by the City of Cottonwood's Building Division. Cosmetic work — replacing countertops, cabinet refacing, or installing new flooring — typically does not require a permit. However, any work that involves moving or adding plumbing fixtures, relocating electrical circuits or outlets, altering the layout of a kitchen island, or making structural modifications will require a building permit. We handle all permit applications and coordinate inspections with the City of Cottonwood as part of every project where permits are needed. You will not be left to navigate the permitting process on your own.
How far is Cottonwood from your Prescott office?
Cottonwood is approximately 38 to 40 miles from our Prescott location, depending on your specific address. The primary route is SR-89A, which passes through Jerome on its way down into the Verde Valley. Drive time is typically 50 to 65 minutes depending on traffic and road conditions. We make this drive regularly for consultations, project management visits, and installation work, and we do not charge a travel fee for Cottonwood clients. If you are in the broader Verde Valley area — Clarkdale, Jerome, Cornville, or Camp Verde — reach out and we can confirm coverage for your location.
What materials hold up best in a vacation rental kitchen or bathroom?
For vacation rental kitchens and bathrooms in Cottonwood, we consistently recommend quartz countertops over natural stone because quartz is non-porous and does not require sealing — an important maintenance consideration when guests turn over every few days. LVP flooring is our top flooring recommendation for rentals because it is completely waterproof, scratch-resistant, and able to withstand rolling luggage, dropped items, and high foot traffic without showing wear. In the shower, groutless solid-surface systems eliminate the most common maintenance complaint in rental bathrooms — stained or deteriorating grout. These three material choices together dramatically reduce the ongoing maintenance burden while keeping the space looking sharp for years.
Can you do a full kitchen and bathroom renovation in an older Cottonwood home?
Absolutely. Older homes in Old Town Cottonwood and the West Cottonwood neighborhoods from the 1970s and 1980s often present unique considerations — older plumbing configurations, galvanized or copper supply lines, original cast-iron or steel drain systems, and kitchen layouts that predate modern appliance standards. Our team has extensive experience navigating the realities of older construction. We assess the existing systems during the initial consultation, identify anything that may need to be brought up to current code before the cosmetic work begins, and give you a complete picture of the project scope before any work starts. We do not believe in surprises, and we have the experience to anticipate the issues that older Cottonwood homes typically present.
