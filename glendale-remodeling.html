Glendale Neighborhoods

Remodeling Across Glendale's Neighborhoods

Historic Downtown Glendale & Catlin Court

The neighborhoods surrounding Glendale's historic downtown — Catlin Court, Murphy Park, the antique district — have some of the most character-rich housing in the West Valley. Homes from the 1940s through 1970s carry architectural details that newer construction can't replicate, but original kitchens and bathrooms that need full rebuilds. We work carefully with older construction: checking for outdated wiring, galvanized plumbing, and asbestos-era materials before recommending scope.

Arrowhead Ranch

Arrowhead Ranch is one of Glendale's most established upscale communities, with homes built from the 1980s through early 2000s. The older homes here have kitchens and bathrooms from 20–35 years ago that are ready for full modernization. Master bathroom conversions — tub removal, custom walk-in shower, double vanity — are the most common project in this area. Kitchen remodels with custom or semi-custom cabinetry and stone countertops follow closely.

Westgate & Camelback Ranch Area

The newer sections of Glendale near Westgate and Camelback Ranch have more recent construction with builder-grade finishes. Countertop and cabinet upgrades are the most cost-effective improvement in these homes, and LVP flooring replacements (replacing dated tile or carpet) make a large visual impact at a reasonable price per square foot.

Common Questions

Remodeling in Glendale — FAQs

Do you remodel older Glendale homes from the 1950s or 1960s?

Yes, and we approach them differently than newer construction. Older homes often have galvanized pipes, outdated electrical panels, and subfloor issues that need to be assessed before final scope is set. We walk through the home thoroughly before writing a contract, so the estimate reflects what's actually there — not assumptions based on a newer build.

Who handles permits and inspections?

We do. We handle all required permits and inspections for your Glendale project as part of our standard process.

What's the most popular bathroom remodel in Glendale?

The tub-to-shower conversion is the most requested bathroom project in Glendale — removing an unused tub, rebuilding the shower pan, and creating a walk-in shower with either tile or a groutless solid-surface system. Master bathrooms with double vanities and frameless glass enclosures are also highly requested in Arrowhead Ranch and similar neighborhoods.

How soon can you start a project in Glendale?

Our current schedule typically runs 3–6 weeks out from contract signing. That lead time is used to order materials (especially custom cabinets, which take 6–10 weeks from order to delivery) so that when demo day arrives, all materials are staged and ready. We'll discuss realistic timelines during your free consultation.
