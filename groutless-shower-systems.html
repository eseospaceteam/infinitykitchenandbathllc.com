Groutless Shower Systems in Prescott, AZ

Solid-surface shower walls that look stunning, clean in seconds, and last a lifetime — backed by a full manufacturer and installation warranty.

Transform Your Bathroom

Say Goodbye to Grout — Forever

Traditional tile showers look beautiful when new — but give them a few months and the grout becomes a constant battle. Mold, mildew, staining, and endless scrubbing. It doesn't have to be this way.

Our groutless solid-surface shower systems are seamlessly installed around your tub and shower, with no grout lines for water, mold, or bacteria to hide in. The surface is smooth, non-porous, and cleans with nothing more than a damp cloth.

Best of all, they're available in dozens of colors, patterns, and finishes — from minimalist white panels to dramatic marble-look designs that make a real statement.

Why Choose Groutless

The Smarter Shower Wall Solution

Zero Maintenance Grout

No grout lines means no scrubbing, no sealing, and no re-grouting. Ever. Just wipe it down and you're done.

Mold & Mildew Resistant

The seamless, non-porous surface gives mold and mildew nowhere to hide. Your shower stays fresh and hygienic without the effort.

Dozens of Designs

From crisp white to bold marbled patterns, our solid-surface systems come in styles to match any bathroom aesthetic.

Fast Installation

Less disruption, faster completion. Our groutless systems are engineered for efficient installation — you'll be enjoying your new shower sooner.

Full Lifetime Warranty

Both Infinity Kitchen and Bath and our manufacturer stand behind these systems with complete lifetime warranty coverage. It's built to last.

Affordable Luxury

The look and feel of premium tile at a fraction of the long-term cost — no re-grouting, no sealing, no contractor callbacks down the road.
