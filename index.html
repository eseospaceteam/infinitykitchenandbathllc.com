Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Infinity Kitchens and Baths offer?

We handle full kitchen and bathroom remodels, custom countertops, cabinets, tile and walk-in showers, LVP flooring, aging-in-place modifications, and whole-house remodeling for homeowners across Prescott and the Quad Cities.

How much does a kitchen or bathroom remodel cost in Prescott?

Costs vary with size, materials, and scope. As a general guide, most bathroom remodels in the Prescott area range from about $12,000 to $30,000, and full kitchen remodels from roughly $25,000 to $60,000 or more. We provide a detailed, fixed quote after a free in-home consultation — see our kitchen and bathroom cost guides for local pricing breakdowns.

Is Infinity Kitchens and Baths licensed and insured?

Yes. Infinity Kitchens and Baths is a licensed Arizona contractor (AZ ROC #339999), bonded, and fully insured. Every crew member is background-checked and drug-tested.

How long does a remodel take?

Most bathroom remodels take about two to four weeks, and full kitchen remodels about three to six weeks from the first day of demolition, depending on scope and material lead times. We give you a week-by-week schedule before work begins.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Every project begins with a free, no-obligation in-home consultation and design assessment. Call 928-800-1998 to schedule.
