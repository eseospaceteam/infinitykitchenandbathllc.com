The Right Cabinets Make Your Kitchen Work

Cabinets are the backbone of every kitchen — they define how space is used, how the room feels, and whether your day-to-day cooking experience is smooth or frustrating. The wrong layout, poor-quality box construction, or doors that don't close properly will remind you of that mistake every single morning. The right cabinets, on the other hand, make your kitchen feel effortless. That's what Infinity Kitchens and Baths designs and installs for homeowners across Prescott and the surrounding Quad Cities.

Unlike big-box stores where you're buying off a warehouse shelf, Infinity sources cabinets factory-direct. That means you get better construction — plywood boxes, soft-close hardware, and dovetail drawer joints as standard — at a price that's competitive with (or lower than) what the home improvement chains charge for inferior particleboard products. Our factory relationships let us offer semi-custom and full custom lines that wouldn't otherwise be accessible without going through a high-end designer showroom.

Before a single cabinet is ordered, our team creates a full CAD layout of your kitchen. You'll see exactly how the new cabinets will look — where every upper, lower, and tall unit sits, how the corner solutions work, and how the overall design flows with your countertops and flooring. This design-first approach is part of our full kitchen remodeling process, and it's one of the main reasons our clients are consistently surprised by how smoothly installation day goes. There are no surprises because everything has been planned in advance.

Cabinet Options We Install

Not every kitchen or budget calls for the same solution. Infinity works with all cabinet categories so you get the best match for your project scope, timeline, and goals.

Stock Cabinets

Available in standard fixed sizes, stock cabinets ship quickly and cost the least. They're a practical choice for tight budgets or straightforward kitchen layouts where standard dimensions work without modification.

Semi-Custom Cabinets

A wider range of widths, heights, and door styles than stock, with typical lead times of two to four weeks. Semi-custom is where most Prescott homeowners land — you get real design flexibility without the price tag of fully custom work.

Custom Cabinets

Built to your exact specifications — any dimension, any wood species, any finish. Custom cabinets are ideal for unusual kitchen shapes, unique design visions, or high-end remodels where precision and premium materials are non-negotiable.

Frameless (European) Cabinets

Frameless construction means the door covers the entire cabinet opening — no face frame visible. The result is a clean, modern aesthetic with a fully accessible interior opening. Hardware installation is also more straightforward, which keeps labor costs in check.

Face-Frame (Traditional) Cabinets

The classic American cabinet style, with a solid wood frame attached to the front of the box. Face-frame construction adds rigidity and works beautifully with both inset and overlay door styles. It's the look most Prescott homeowners associate with a well-built kitchen.

Cabinet Refacing

If your existing cabinet boxes are structurally sound, refacing lets you replace just the doors, drawer fronts, and visible veneer surfaces. It's a fraction of the cost of a full cabinet replacement and can deliver a completely fresh look in a matter of days rather than weeks.

What Makes a Quality Cabinet — and How to Tell the Difference

The cabinet door is what you see. The box is what you live with. Most quality problems in kitchen cabinets — sagging shelves, swollen bottoms, drawers that stick after a few years — trace back to how the box is built, not the finish on the door. That's why Infinity insists on plywood box construction across its cabinet lines.

Plywood holds screws better than particleboard, resists moisture, and won't sag under the weight of a fully loaded pot-and-pan cabinet. Particleboard is cheaper to manufacture, which is why it shows up in big-box and budget lines, but it expands when it gets wet and loses its grip on hardware over time. In a kitchen — where steam, spills, and temperature swings are a fact of life — the difference matters.

Beyond box material, look at the drawers. Dovetail joinery at the corners means the drawer box is mechanically interlocked, not just glued and stapled. Pair that with undermount soft-close drawer slides and full-extension travel, and you have drawers that open smoothly and quietly every time — even years down the road. Soft-close door hinges complete the picture, protecting cabinet faces from slamming and extending the life of both the hinge and the door.

Our team walks every client through construction specs during the design consultation, so you can compare apples to apples when evaluating quotes from different contractors.

6 Quality Checkpoints to Ask Any Cabinet Contractor

  • 01Are the cabinet boxes made from plywood or particleboard?
  • 02Are drawer boxes constructed with dovetail joints?
  • 03Do all drawers include full-extension, soft-close slides?
  • 04Are soft-close hinges standard on all doors?
  • 05What finish warranty does the cabinet manufacturer provide?
  • 06Will I see a CAD layout before any cabinets are ordered?

Smart Storage Built Into Every Kitchen We Design

A kitchen remodel is the best — and often only — opportunity to fix storage problems at the source. Infinity's design team plans storage solutions into the cabinet layout from day one, not as afterthoughts.

Pull-Out Shelves

Convert deep base cabinets from black holes into organized storage. Pull-out shelves bring everything to you — no more crouching and digging to reach the back corner.

Lazy Susans & Corner Solutions

Corner cabinets are notoriously hard to use. Lazy Susan turntables, blind-corner pull-outs, and magic-corner systems reclaim that space and make it genuinely accessible.

Deep Drawer Banks

Three-drawer base cabinets instead of a door-and-shelf unit make pots, pans, and heavy items far easier to access. Deep drawers are one of the most popular upgrades our clients choose once they see them in the CAD layout.

Tall Pantry Cabinets

Floor-to-ceiling pantry units provide massive storage in a small footprint. We design them with adjustable shelving, pull-out inserts, and built-in organization so every inch earns its keep.

Under-Cabinet Lighting

LED strip lighting mounted under upper cabinets illuminates your countertop work surface and adds visual depth to the kitchen. It's planned during cabinet layout so wiring is hidden inside the cabinet run — no surface conduit.

Built-In Spice & Tray Dividers

Narrow pull-out spice cabinets beside the range and vertical tray dividers for baking sheets and cutting boards keep the most-used items right where you need them without cluttering drawer space.

How We Design & Install Your Kitchen Cabinets

Every Infinity cabinet project follows the same proven process — from first conversation to final walkthrough. No surprises, no shortcuts.

01

Free Kitchen Design Consultation & CAD Layout

We start by measuring your existing kitchen and listening to how you use the space. What storage is missing? What workflow feels awkward? What aesthetic are you going for? From there, our designer builds a full CAD layout so you can see your new kitchen — with exact cabinet placement, door swings, and dimensions — before anything is ordered or demolished. Adjustments at this stage cost nothing.

02

Cabinet Line Selection & Finish Choices

Once the layout is approved, we walk you through door styles, wood species or thermofoil finishes, hardware options, and interior accessories. Our factory-direct sourcing means you're choosing from a broader and better-quality selection than what's available at a big-box retailer. We place the order only after you've signed off on every detail.

03

Demo & Wall Prep

Our crew handles the removal of existing cabinets carefully to protect adjacent surfaces. Walls are inspected and patched, plumbing and electrical are confirmed or relocated if needed, and the space is prepared to receive the new installation. We also coordinate with our countertop and backsplash teams at this stage so the overall project timeline stays on track.

04

Cabinet Installation & Leveling

Cabinet installation starts with establishing a level reference line across the entire room. Upper cabinets are hung first, then base cabinets are set and shimmed to a perfectly level plane. Every unit is secured to wall studs, every face frame is aligned and flush, and every door is adjusted before we move on. Precision here determines whether your countertops will sit flat and your doors will close properly for the next 20 years.

05

Hardware, Trim & Final Walkthrough

Pulls, knobs, and hinges are installed and adjusted. Crown molding, light rail, and toe kicks are fitted and finished. Under-cabinet lighting is connected. Then we walk through the entire kitchen with you — opening every drawer, testing every door, reviewing every storage feature — to confirm everything works exactly as designed. We don't consider a job done until you're satisfied.

Kitchen Cabinet FAQ

How long does cabinet installation take?

For most Prescott kitchens, the physical installation of cabinets takes one to three days once materials arrive. The overall project timeline — including design, ordering, delivery, demo, installation, and finishing — typically runs four to eight weeks depending on the cabinet line chosen and the scope of related work (countertops, backsplash, flooring). Custom cabinets have longer lead times than semi-custom or stock options, and we'll give you a detailed schedule during the consultation.

What's the difference between semi-custom and custom cabinets?

Semi-custom cabinets are manufactured in a range of pre-set sizes (typically in 3-inch width increments) with a wider selection of door styles, finishes, and interior options than stock. They offer excellent value and fit the vast majority of kitchen layouts. Full custom cabinets are built to any dimension you specify — exact width, height, depth — in any wood species or finish. They're ideal for unusual kitchen shapes or high-end projects where absolute precision is required. Infinity can help you determine which tier is right for your project and budget.

Should I choose plywood or particleboard cabinets?

Plywood, without question. Plywood holds fasteners more securely, handles moisture far better, and is significantly more durable over the life of the cabinet. The kitchen environment — steam from cooking, occasional spills, temperature swings — is hard on cabinet boxes. Particleboard swells when it gets wet, strips its screw holes faster, and sags under heavy loads. Infinity specifies plywood box construction across the cabinet lines we install, and we can show you exactly what that looks like side-by-side during your consultation.

Can I reface my existing cabinets instead of replacing them?

Yes — if the existing cabinet boxes are structurally sound (no water damage, no failing joints, no sagging shelves), cabinet refacing is an excellent option. We replace the doors, drawer fronts, and apply new veneer to all exposed box surfaces. You get a completely transformed kitchen aesthetic in a fraction of the time and cost of a full replacement. We assess your existing cabinets during the initial consultation and give you an honest recommendation on whether refacing makes sense or whether the boxes need to be replaced.

What cabinet door styles are most popular in Prescott kitchens?

Shaker remains the most popular style in Prescott — its clean, recessed-panel profile works equally well in traditional, transitional, and modern kitchens. Flat-panel (slab) doors are increasingly popular for contemporary designs. Raised-panel doors continue to appeal to homeowners who prefer a more traditional or formal aesthetic. In terms of finish, painted white and soft gray are perennial favorites, with natural wood tones (particularly maple and oak) seeing a strong resurgence as homeowners embrace warmer, organic interiors.
