FAQ

Kitchen Remodeling in Chino Valley — Common Questions

Do you remodel kitchens in Chino Valley?

Yes — Chino Valley is part of our core service area. We remodel kitchens across Old Home Manor, Chino Meadows, Territorial, and the rural and horse-property homes north of Prescott, with no travel fee since we're only about 20 minutes away.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Chino Valley?

Most Chino Valley kitchen remodels take about 4–8 weeks of active construction. Cabinet lead times are usually the biggest variable, so we order early and give you a written schedule before demolition begins. A lighter refresh — new countertops, backsplash, and hardware — wraps up faster.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Chino Valley?

Most mid-range Chino Valley kitchen remodels run $25,000–$50,000. A minor refresh typically falls between $8,000 and $18,000, while high-end kitchens with custom cabinetry and premium stone run $55,000–$100,000 or more. We provide a detailed written estimate after a free in-home consultation.

Do you handle permits and where do you buy materials?

Yes. When a project requires a permit, we handle the application and inspections for you as part of the job. We buy cabinets, countertops, and finishes factory-direct, which keeps our pricing roughly 15–25% below typical retail and gives you showroom-quality materials without the markup.
