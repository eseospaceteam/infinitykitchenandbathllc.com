FAQ

Kitchen Remodeling in Dewey-Humboldt — Common Questions

Do you remodel kitchens in Dewey-Humboldt?

Yes — Dewey-Humboldt is part of our regular service area. We remodel kitchens across Prescott Country Club, Mountain Pines, and the homes tucked between Prescott Valley and the Bradshaw foothills, with no travel fee since we're only about 25 minutes away in Prescott.

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Dewey-Humboldt?

Most Dewey-Humboldt kitchen remodels take about 4–8 weeks of active construction. Cabinet lead times are usually the biggest variable, so we order early and give you a written schedule before demolition begins. A lighter refresh — new countertops, backsplash, and hardware — wraps up faster.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Dewey-Humboldt?

Most mid-range Dewey-Humboldt kitchen remodels run $25,000–$50,000. A minor refresh typically falls between $8,000 and $18,000, while high-end kitchens with custom cabinetry and premium stone run $55,000–$100,000 or more. We provide a detailed written estimate after a free in-home consultation.

Do you handle permits and where do you buy materials?

Yes. When a project requires a permit, we handle the application and inspections for you as part of the job. We buy cabinets, countertops, and finishes factory-direct, which keeps our pricing roughly 15–25% below typical retail and gives you showroom-quality materials without the markup.
