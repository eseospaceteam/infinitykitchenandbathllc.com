Kitchen Remodeling in Peoria — Common Questions

What are the most common Peoria kitchen projects in Vistancia?

In Vistancia the most common projects are countertop and cabinet upgrades, under-cabinet lighting, and backsplash — replacing builder-grade finishes with quartz, new cabinetry, and tile. Full open-concept remodels are common in older Arrowhead Lakes homes.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Peoria?

Most mid-range Peoria kitchen remodels run $18,000–$45,000; a refresh $8,000–$18,000 and high-end custom kitchens $55,000+. You get a detailed written estimate after a free consultation.

How long does a Peoria kitchen remodel take?

Most Peoria kitchen remodels take about 4–8 weeks of active construction. Cabinet lead time is the biggest variable, so we order early and give you a written schedule before demolition.
