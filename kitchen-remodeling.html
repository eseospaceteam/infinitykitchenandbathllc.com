Expert Kitchen Renovations

Your Dream Kitchen Starts Here

The kitchen is the heart of the home — and it should look and work like it. At Infinity Kitchen and Bath, we design and build kitchens that are as practical as they are beautiful.

Whether you want a full kitchen overhaul or targeted improvements, our team has the expertise to make it happen. We focus on functionality first — especially the classic kitchen work triangle between your sink, oven, and refrigerator — then layer in the design details that make the space uniquely yours.

Our process starts with a free in-home consultation where we use computer-aided design to show you exactly what your new kitchen will look like before a single cabinet is removed.

Our Approach

Design That Works for How You Live

A beautiful kitchen is only half the goal. We design around how you actually use your space — your cooking habits, your family's flow, your storage needs.

  • We recommend selecting appliances early — they inform color schemes and cabinet sizing
  • We optimize the kitchen work triangle — sink, oven, and refrigerator placement
  • Floor plan is developed before wallpaper, backsplash, and accent decisions
  • We stay current on trends and can guide you toward timeless or modern looks
  • Budget-conscious recommendations without sacrificing quality or style
