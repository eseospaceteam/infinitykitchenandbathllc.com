Luxury Vinyl Plank Flooring in Prescott, AZ

100% waterproof, scratch-resistant, and beautiful — LVP flooring is the smarter choice for kitchens, bathrooms, and whole-home installations.

Modern Flooring Solutions

Beautiful Floors That Are Built to Last

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP) flooring has become the top choice for Prescott homeowners — and for good reason. It looks like real hardwood, feels comfortable underfoot, and stands up to everything Arizona life throws at it.

Unlike hardwood, LVP is 100% waterproof, making it ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and any other space where moisture is a concern. It's also highly resistant to scratches, dents, and everyday wear — perfect for active households and pets.

We carry a wide selection of styles, from classic warm wood tones to modern grey planks and everything in between. Our team will help you find the perfect look for your home and install it flawlessly.

Why LVP?

The Advantages of Luxury Vinyl Plank

100% Waterproof

True waterproof construction — not just water-resistant. Safe for kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, and any area prone to spills or moisture.

Scratch & Dent Resistant

A tough wear layer protects against everyday scratches, scuffs, and dents. Great for households with kids, pets, or just regular use.

Realistic Wood & Stone Looks

High-definition printing technology creates surfaces virtually indistinguishable from real hardwood or stone — at a fraction of the cost.

Easy Installation

Click-lock installation means less time on the job and lower installation costs. We can often complete whole-room installations in a single day.

Cost-Effective

All the beauty of hardwood at a significantly lower price point. Our factory-direct sourcing saves you even more without sacrificing quality.

Easy to Maintain

No sanding, no refinishing, no special cleaners. Just sweep and mop. LVP holds its finish and beauty for years with minimal effort.
