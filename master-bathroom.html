Your Master Bath Should Feel Like a Personal Retreat

Of every room in the home, the master bathroom is the one homeowners feel most strongly about — and return to every single morning and evening. It is the place where the day begins and ends, and when it falls short of your expectations, that frustration compounds over time. A cramped layout, a single vanity for two people, outdated fixtures, or a shower that barely functions can chip away at daily comfort in ways that no other room quite matches.

What separates a merely functional master bathroom from one that truly feels luxurious comes down to a handful of intentional choices: a space plan that accommodates two people without congestion, a layered lighting scheme that transitions from bright task lighting in the morning to soft ambient warmth at night, a heated tile floor underfoot on cold Prescott mornings, and a double vanity with dedicated storage for each person. These are not extravagances — they are the features that homeowners in Prescott tell us they wish they had done years earlier.

At Infinity Kitchens and Baths, our approach to master bathroom remodeling begins with a free in-home design consultation and a complimentary CAD rendering so you can see your new bathroom before a single tile is removed. We handle design, material selection, demo, waterproofing, tile, plumbing, electrical, and final finish in a single coordinated build — no subcontractor juggling, no responsibility gaps. Freestanding Soaking Tub

A freestanding soaking tub is the single most powerful visual element you can add to a master bathroom. Positioned against a tile accent wall or centered under a window, it transforms the room into something that feels genuinely hotel-caliber. We help you select the right tub profile — oval, rectangular, or sculpted — and coordinate the floor filler and drain finish to complete the look.

Heated Tile Floor

Radiant heat beneath porcelain or natural stone tile is one of the most popular upgrades in Prescott master baths — and one of the least visible. An electric mat is installed under the tile during the remodel, connected to a programmable thermostat you can set to warm the floor before your alarm goes off. On a cold Prescott morning, there is nothing quite like it.

Large-Format Tile

24×24 and 24×48 format tiles on both floors and walls create a dramatically cleaner look than standard sizes — fewer grout lines mean a more expansive, spa-like feel and far easier maintenance. We work with rectified porcelain, marble-look porcelain, and natural stone, and our installers are experienced in the precision leveling that large-format tile demands.

Spa Lighting Design

Great lighting is the single most underestimated element of master bathroom design. We design layered schemes that combine recessed ambient lighting, vertical task fixtures flanking or framing the vanity mirror, and accent lighting at the shower or tub niche. Dimmer controls let you shift the mood from bright and energizing in the morning to calm and relaxing in the evening.

How We Remodel Your Master Bathroom

Our process is designed to be transparent, efficient, and stress-free from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

01

In-Home Design Consultation & CAD Rendering

We meet you at your home, measure the existing space, discuss your goals and budget, and walk you through material options. Within a few days we deliver a complimentary CAD rendering so you can visualize the finished bathroom before committing to a single line item. No pressure, no obligation.

02

Fixture, Tile & Material Selection

We guide you through tile selection, vanity configurations, countertop materials, shower enclosure styles, plumbing fixtures, and lighting. Our team coordinates everything so that finishes and dimensions work together cohesively — no last-minute surprises when materials arrive on-site.

03

Demo & Waterproofing

Careful demolition protects what stays while fully removing what goes. Once the substrate is exposed, we apply a continuous waterproofing membrane to all wet areas — shower walls, shower floor, and tub deck — before any tile is set. Proper waterproofing is the single most important step in a long-lasting bathroom remodel.

04

Tile, Vanity, Shower & Plumbing

Floor tile goes down first, including radiant heat mat installation if selected. Shower tile and enclosure follow, then vanity cabinet installation, countertop fabrication and set, and plumbing rough-in and trim. Every trade works in a planned sequence so the project moves efficiently without rework.

05

Lighting, Hardware & Final Walkthrough

Electrical fixtures, vanity lighting, and any accent or recessed lighting are installed and connected. Hardware — towel bars, robe hooks, toilet paper holders, shower handles — is set to match your finish selection. We do a detailed final walkthrough with you, address any punch-list items on the spot, and don't consider the job done until you're fully satisfied.

Master Bathroom FAQ

Common questions from Prescott homeowners planning a master bathroom remodel.

How long does a master bathroom remodel take in Prescott?
A typical master bathroom remodel in Prescott takes between three and five weeks from the start of demolition to the final walkthrough, depending on scope. Projects that involve moving plumbing, adding radiant heat, or a significant layout change may run closer to six weeks. Our CAD design and pre-ordering process keeps the schedule tight — materials are staged before demo begins so there are no delays waiting on tile or fixtures.
Should I keep a tub in the master bath?
If you are planning to sell within the next few years, the conventional wisdom has long been to keep a tub — but that guidance applies more to homes where the master is the only full bath. If you have a tub in a secondary bathroom, converting the master tub to a large walk-in shower is a popular and high-value choice. For buyers who use soaking tubs regularly, a freestanding tub as a design statement (rather than a built-in surround) is the better investment. We'll help you think through this decision during your consultation.
What tile is most popular for master bathrooms?
Large-format porcelain tile — particularly marble-look and concrete-look options in 24×24 or 24×48 sizes — is currently the most requested choice among Prescott homeowners. It photographs beautifully, requires minimal grout maintenance, and holds up exceptionally well in a wet environment. For shower floors, a smaller mosaic or 4×12 format tile provides the grout lines needed for slip resistance. Natural stone is also popular for clients seeking a warmer, more organic look, and we work with travertine, marble, and slate regularly.
How much does a master bathroom remodel cost in Prescott?
Master bathroom remodels in Prescott typically range from $18,000 to $45,000 depending on the size of the space, the extent of layout changes, and the material selections. A mid-range remodel with a new double vanity, walk-in shower, updated tile, and new fixtures generally falls between $22,000 and $32,000. Larger spaces with freestanding tubs, premium stone tile, and radiant heat can reach or exceed $45,000. We provide detailed, line-item estimates after the design consultation — no vague ballparks.
Can you expand my master bathroom into an adjacent closet?
Yes — borrowing space from a walk-in closet or an adjoining room is one of the most effective ways to create a truly spa-scale master bathroom without a home addition. We assess the structural implications, plumbing routing, and permit requirements during the design consultation. Many Prescott master bathrooms have been meaningfully enlarged this way, gaining room for a freestanding tub, a larger shower, or a dedicated dressing area that simply wasn't possible in the original footprint.
