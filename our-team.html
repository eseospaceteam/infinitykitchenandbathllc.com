What to Ask Any Contractor

Before hiring any remodeling contractor in Prescott — including us — ask these questions:

  1. Can I see your ROC license number and verify it at azroc.gov?
  2. Are you bonded and insured — can you provide a certificate?
  3. Do you pull permits for work that requires them?
  4. Is your quote fixed-price or time-and-materials?
  5. Who physically does the work — your own employees or subcontractors?
  6. Do you offer a written workmanship warranty?

We pass every one of these checks. Any contractor who can't answer them clearly is a risk to your project and your home.
