The upgrades Prescott Lakes owners ask for most are quartz countertops, lighter refaced or repainted cabinetry, opened-up layouts, and spa-style master baths.

Updating Prescott Lakes Homes

The highest-impact Prescott Lakes projects tend to be countertop and cabinet refreshes and master-bath upgrades. Swapping early-2000s tile or granite for quartz, refacing or repainting solid oak and cherry cabinets to lighter modern tones, and converting a builder tub-and-shower master bath into a curbless walk-in shower with dual vanities transform how the home looks and lives — and they protect resale value in a community where buyers expect updated finishes.

One local consideration: Prescott Lakes is an HOA community with architectural guidelines for anything visible from the exterior. Interior kitchen and bath remodels are typically unaffected, but if your project touches windows, exterior doors, or the building envelope, we’ll help keep it within the community’s standards and handle the required City of Prescott permits and inspections.

FAQ

Prescott Lakes Remodeling — Common Questions

Do you remodel homes in Prescott Lakes?

Yes — Prescott Lakes is a core service area. We remodel kitchens and bathrooms across all of its sub-neighborhoods, from townhomes to custom estate homes, and we're only a short drive away with no travel fee.

My Prescott Lakes home is from the early 2000s — is it worth remodeling?

Almost always. Early-2000s Prescott Lakes homes are structurally solid but carry dated finishes — tile or first-generation granite counters, oak or cherry cabinets, cultured-marble vanities. Refreshing counters, cabinets, and the master bath dramatically updates the home and protects resale value, usually without touching the layout or footprint.

Does the Prescott Lakes HOA affect my remodel?

Only exterior-visible work. Interior kitchen and bathroom remodels are typically unaffected by HOA architectural review. If your project changes windows, doors, or anything visible from outside, we help keep it within community guidelines and handle the City of Prescott permits and inspections.

How much does a remodel cost in Prescott Lakes?

Most mid-range Prescott Lakes kitchen remodels run $25,000–$50,000 and bathrooms $15,000–$35,000, with higher-end finishes common in this community. You'll get a detailed written estimate after a free in-home consultation.
