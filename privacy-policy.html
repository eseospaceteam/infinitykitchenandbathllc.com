Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 — Infinity Kitchen and Bath LLC

Infinity Kitchen and Bath LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website infinitykitchenandbathllc.com (the “Site”). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you visit our Site or contact us about our remodeling services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you fill out a contact form, request a consultation, or communicate with us by phone or email, you may provide:

  • Your name and contact information (phone, email, address)
  • Details about your remodeling project or home
  • Any other information you choose to share

We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, and provide our remodeling services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect certain technical information through standard web server logs and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), including:

  • Your IP address and general geographic location
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time on site, and referring URL
  • Date and time of your visit

This information is used in aggregate to understand how visitors use our Site and to improve our content. It is not linked to personally identifiable information.

Cookies

Our Site may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience. Analytics tools like Google Analytics use cookies to track aggregate site usage. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Doing so may affect certain features of the Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Provide remodeling services and communicate about your project
  • Send follow-up information you have requested
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors find us
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We will not use your contact information to send unsolicited marketing communications beyond responses to your direct inquiries, unless you have explicitly opted in.

How We Protect Your Information

We implement reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our Site. We retain contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill our business purposes and legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party service providers to operate our website, communicate with customers, or analyze site performance. These providers include:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Email service providers — for responding to inquiries
  • Hosting providers — for operating and maintaining the Site

These third parties are contractually restricted from using your information for purposes other than those described. We do not currently integrate with advertising platforms, remarketing services, or social media tracking pixels.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the information below. We will respond to such requests within a reasonable time. If you have received communications from us and wish to opt out of further contact, simply reply to any email or call us and we will honor your request promptly.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
