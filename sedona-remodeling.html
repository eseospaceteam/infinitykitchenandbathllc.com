Luxury Remodeling for Sedona's World-Class Homes

Sedona occupies one of the most visually spectacular positions in all of North America. Surrounded by towering red sandstone formations — Cathedral Rock, Bell Rock, Courthouse Butte — it is a place where the landscape sets an extraordinarily high bar. Homes here, whether primary residences in the Uptown area or luxury vacation rentals in the Village of Oak Creek, should be designed and finished to a level equal to their setting. A kitchen or bathroom that lags behind the rest of the property's character is a missed opportunity — and in a market where Sedona homes routinely list above $800,000, every interior detail matters.

Remodeling in Sedona calls for a specific vocabulary of materials. Natural stone and quartzite countertops that echo the warm ochres and rusts of the red-rock landscape. Large-format tile referencing the earthy, organic palette that makes Sedona interiors feel of-place rather than imported. Walk-in showers with frameless glass that keep sightlines open and uncluttered — an aesthetic priority in homes where the surroundings are the view. Outdoor kitchens and covered entertainment spaces that take full advantage of Sedona's nearly year-round outdoor living climate, where temperatures sit comfortably for al fresco dining well into November and resume early in March.

Primary Residences & Investment Properties

Remodeling for Sedona's Luxury and Vacation Rental Market

Primary Residences: Materials That Belong in Sedona

Sedona homeowners who live in the community year-round or part-time consistently want interiors that feel like they belong to the landscape, not imported from Phoenix or Scottsdale. That translates directly into material choices: quartzite countertops in warm ivory, gold, and rust tones that echo the red-rock formations visible from nearly every vantage point in the city. White oak cabinetry with a visible natural grain that feels organic rather than lacquered. Concrete-look or natural stone tile that grounds a space without competing with the views outside. These are not just aesthetic preferences — they are what holds value in a market where buyers are paying for the Sedona experience as much as the square footage.

Infinity sources these premium natural materials factory-direct, which means our pricing on quartzite slabs, stone tile, and custom cabinetry is consistently lower than what Sedona's local design showrooms charge. You get the same quality — often from the same stone yards and cabinet manufacturers — at a better price, with licensed installation handled by our experienced crew.

Vacation Rental Remodels: Beauty That Earns Five-Star Reviews

Sedona is one of Arizona's most active short-term rental markets, drawing nearly three million visitors per year. Airbnb, VRBO, and luxury vacation rental agencies list thousands of Sedona properties, and the kitchen and bathroom quality of a listing directly drives its nightly rate and occupancy. Investors who upgrade a dated kitchen or bathroom consistently see meaningful increases in booking revenue — not just aesthetically, but in the listing photography that drives clicks in a competitive market.

For vacation rental bathrooms, we strongly recommend groutless shower systems. Traditional tile grout is the first thing that shows wear in a high-turnover rental environment — it stains, discolors between guests, and eventually requires costly re-grouting. Groutless solid-surface shower panels eliminate that maintenance problem entirely. They look resort-quality, clean in minutes, and hold up to the heavy use that comes with a well-booked vacation rental. It is one of the smartest investments a Sedona rental owner can make, and it is one of our most requested upgrades for the market.

We also design Sedona vacation rental kitchens for both visual impact and durability — quartz or quartzite countertops that resist staining, durable hardware finishes, and cabinet configurations that photograph as well as they function. First impressions from a listing photo translate directly to bookings.

Does Infinity Kitchen & Bath travel to Sedona for remodeling projects?

What countertop materials work best for Sedona's aesthetic?

For Sedona homes, natural quartzite is our most popular countertop recommendation. Quartzite slabs in warm ivory, gold-veined, and rust-toned colorways complement the red-rock landscape palette in a way that feels intentional rather than generic. Marble works beautifully in bathroom applications. For clients who want the natural stone look with slightly higher durability in a busy kitchen, we also offer premium quartz in natural stone patterns. We bring physical samples to every in-home consultation so you can see how the material reads in your specific space and light.

What are the best bathroom features for a Sedona vacation rental?

Vacation rental bathrooms in Sedona should balance visual luxury with practical durability. Our top recommendations: groutless shower panels (solid surface or large-format slabs) that eliminate grout maintenance between guest stays; frameless glass shower enclosures that photograph well and make the space feel larger; vessel sinks or floating vanities that read as high-end in listing photos; and durable, easy-clean finishes throughout. A walk-in shower that looks resort-quality will directly improve your listing's star rating and nightly rate. Visit our groutless shower systems page for details on the options best suited to high-turnover rental properties.

How much does a luxury kitchen remodel cost in Sedona, AZ?

Sedona's median home value of $800,000-plus means that a well-executed kitchen remodel in this market delivers strong returns in both enjoyment and resale value. Bathroom remodels range from $18,000 for a focused master bath refresh to $60,000 or more for a full primary suite transformation with premium stone tile, custom vanity, and walk-in shower.
