What Makes Us Different

Why Prescott Homeowners Choose Infinity Kitchens and Baths

There are dozens of remodeling contractors in the Prescott area. Here's what genuinely sets us apart.

01

Factory-Direct Material Access at Every Budget Level

Most remodeling contractors purchase materials through distributors, who mark up prices before passing them on. We've built direct relationships with manufacturers and suppliers over 13+ years of business, which means our clients get access to the same premium materials at significantly lower cost. This isn't a promotional claim — it's a structural advantage built into how we do business.

What it means for you in practice: the countertop surface or tile selection that might be out of reach with another contractor is often within budget when you work with us. We bring material samples to your home during the consultation so you can see, touch, and compare real options in your actual space — not just look at them on a website or in a fluorescent-lit showroom. Factory-direct pricing applies across all categories: countertops, tile, shower systems, cabinetry, flooring, and fixtures.

02

One Contractor, Full Accountability from Start to Finish

One of the most common frustrations in home remodeling is dealing with multiple contractors who each have their own schedule, their own scope of responsibility, and a tendency to blame each other when something goes wrong. We eliminate that problem entirely by handling every aspect of your project in-house: design, demolition, plumbing, electrical, tile, cabinetry, countertops, flooring, and finish work.

You have one point of contact throughout your project — a project manager who knows the full scope of your job from the first meeting to the final walkthrough. There are no handoffs to unfamiliar subcontractors at critical stages. There's no confusion about who's responsible when a schedule slips. And there's no situation where a plumber is waiting on a tile setter who's waiting on a cabinet delivery. We sequence and coordinate everything ourselves, and we're accountable for the result in its entirety.

03

Computer-Aided Design: See It Before We Build It

We use computer-aided design software to create a detailed 3D rendering of your finished project before construction begins. This isn't a rough sketch or a mood board — it's a photorealistic walk-through of your new kitchen, bathroom, or living space that shows you exactly how the materials, colors, and layout will look together in your actual home. You can see how the countertop interacts with the backsplash, how the cabinet color reads in your specific lighting, and whether the layout feels open or tight.

This step pays for itself many times over by catching potential problems before they're built in. A client who realizes in the design phase that they'd prefer a different countertop edge profile or a different cabinet layout saves significantly compared to one who discovers their preference after materials have been ordered or installed. We don't charge separately for design services — it's included in our process because we believe making good decisions early is how we deliver projects that clients are consistently happy with years later.

04

Licensed, Bonded & Insured — AZ ROC #339999

Infinity Kitchens and Baths holds Arizona ROC License #339999 and maintains full licensing, bonding, and insurance for all remodeling work throughout the state of Arizona. Every crew member who enters your home is background-checked and drug-tested. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage on every job site — which means you're protected if anything unexpected happens during the project.

In Arizona, contractor licensing is regulated by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). You can verify any contractor's current license status at roc.az.gov. We encourage every homeowner in Prescott to verify this before signing a contract with any contractor — licensed or not. Unlicensed contractors may offer lower bids, but they offer no legal recourse if work is incomplete or substandard, and their work may create disclosure obligations when you sell your home. Licensing isn't a formality — it's a meaningful minimum standard that protects you throughout and after the project.

05

Our Workmanship Warranty & Quality Standards

We stand behind our work with written workmanship warranties on every project. Many of the products we install carry manufacturer warranties ranging from several years to lifetime coverage — including our groutless shower systems, which carry a full lifetime warranty from both the manufacturer and from Infinity Kitchens and Baths directly. Before any project is considered complete, we conduct a thorough final walkthrough with the homeowner present and create a written punch list of any items that need attention.

We don't consider a project done until the homeowner is satisfied — not just tolerant. That means we don't collect final payment until the punch list is fully resolved. This policy reflects a simple belief: our reputation in the Prescott community is built project by project, and the only way to maintain it is to hold ourselves to the standard we'd want if we were the homeowner. We've been doing business here since 2011, and we intend to be here for decades more.

06

Deep Roots in Prescott & Yavapai County

We're not a national franchise or a company that relocated here recently. Infinity Kitchens and Baths was founded in Prescott and has been serving the Prescott area, Prescott Valley, Chino Valley, Dewey-Humboldt, Mayer, and all of Yavapai County since 2011. Our team members live here. We know the building codes and the specific challenges that come with remodeling homes in the Prescott area — older construction methods, occasional asbestos-era materials in homes from the 1970s and 1980s, and the particular demands of Arizona's climate on building materials.

That local knowledge has real value for your project. We know what construction approaches hold up best in our specific climate, and we have established relationships with local suppliers that help keep projects on schedule. When you work with Infinity Kitchens and Baths, you're working with a team that has genuine stakes in this community — and that means we approach your project the way we'd want our own home treated.

Common Questions

Remodeling FAQs

Do you do tile work — showers, floors and backsplashes?

Yes, all three. We build custom tile showers and tub surrounds, install tile flooring throughout the home in porcelain, ceramic, natural stone and wood-look plank, and design tile backsplashes as part of our kitchen remodels. When you remodel with us, the tile work is handled by the same team on the same schedule and under the same warranty.

Do you install tile backsplashes on their own?

A tile backsplash is included in the design whenever we remodel a kitchen. For a standalone backsplash with no other work, we will usually refer you straight to our trusted tile installer instead of sending a sales rep out for a single small job — you get a faster answer and a better price that way. Call us and we will point you in the right direction either way.

Do you install tile flooring as well as luxury vinyl plank?

Both. Tile is harder, fully waterproof and lasts decades but costs more and is colder underfoot. Luxury vinyl plank is warmer, quicker to install and easier on the budget, though it will not match tile for lifespan. We install both and give a straight recommendation for the specific room rather than pushing one product.

How do I get started with Infinity Kitchens and Baths?

The first step is a free in-home consultation. You can schedule one by calling us at 928-800-1998 or filling out the contact form on our website. During the consultation, one of our designers comes to your home, takes measurements, listens to what you're hoping to accomplish, and begins sketching out design ideas. There's no charge, no contract to sign, and no pressure to commit. Most homeowners find that after the consultation they have a much clearer sense of what's possible within their budget and timeline — even if they weren't sure exactly what they wanted going in.

Do you offer financing for remodeling projects?

Yes. We offer financing options for qualifying projects, which allows many homeowners to move forward with the remodel they actually want rather than making compromises on scope or material quality. Financing options are discussed during or after the consultation once we have a project estimate. We work with several financing partners and can walk you through the terms, rates, and what you'd qualify for based on your situation. There's no obligation to use financing — it's simply an option we make available for clients who want it.

How do you handle permits for remodeling projects in Prescott?

When a project requires a permit, we handle it for you. Cosmetic updates within the existing footprint — tile, vanities, countertops, flooring — typically don't require one. We'll tell you upfront during the consultation whether your project will need a permit.

Can you remodel a kitchen and bathroom at the same time?

Yes, and this is often a smart way to approach a renovation if you're planning both. Doing a kitchen and bathroom simultaneously allows our team to work on both projects with one mobilization and coordinate scheduling to minimize disruption. You also benefit from a unified design approach where the materials, finishes, and color palette of both spaces are considered together. Multi-room projects are well within our capabilities — we've managed simultaneous renovations of three and four rooms for clients throughout Prescott, including full whole-house renovations. The consultation process for multi-room projects works the same way: we walk through each space, discuss your goals and budget for each, and develop a plan that sequences the work intelligently.

What areas do you serve beyond Prescott?

We serve all of Yavapai County, including Prescott Valley, Chino Valley, Dewey-Humboldt, Mayer, Cordes Lakes, Williamson Valley, and surrounding communities. If you're outside these areas, call us at 928-800-1998 and we'll let you know whether your location is within our service range. Our base of operations is in Prescott, and we've built most of our project history in the Prescott metro area, but we regularly work throughout the county for the right projects.

What should I look for when hiring a remodeling contractor in Arizona?

Start with licensing. Arizona requires all general contractors to hold a current ROC license — you can verify any contractor's status at roc.az.gov. Ask for the license number before you sign anything. Beyond licensing, look for a contractor who carries both general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage, who will provide a detailed written scope of work and timeline before starting, and who has verifiable reviews from clients in your area rather than just a polished website. Be wary of bids that are dramatically lower than others — unusually low bids often signal unlicensed work, inferior materials, or a contractor who will hit you with change orders once the project is underway. The cheapest bid is rarely the cheapest finished project.
