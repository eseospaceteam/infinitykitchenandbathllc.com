Surprise Neighborhoods

Remodeling Across Surprise's Communities

Marley Park

Marley Park is one of Surprise's most sought-after master-planned communities, with a new-urbanist design, walking trails, and community amenities. Homes built in the 2000s through 2010s are showing their builder-grade origins: laminate countertops, stock cabinet boxes, basic tile. Kitchen and bathroom upgrades here return well — buyers in Marley Park compare home to home carefully, and updated kitchens and bathrooms stand out immediately.

Sun City Grand (55+ Active Adult)

Sun City Grand is a Del Webb active adult community in Surprise with homes built from the late 1990s through the 2010s. Aging-in-place remodeling is the primary need: curbless shower conversions, grab bars anchored to blocking, comfort-height toilets, lever hardware, pull-out kitchen shelves, and wider doorways. We treat these modifications as design projects — they should look intentional and well-executed, not bolted on as afterthoughts.

Greer Ranch & Sterling Grove

Greer Ranch and Sterling Grove represent Surprise's higher-end residential market — larger homes, more premium lots, buyers who expect custom-quality finishes. Full kitchen remodels with custom or semi-custom cabinetry, primary bathroom renovations with large walk-in showers and freestanding tubs, and whole-home flooring replacements are common at this price point. These projects require more detailed design planning, which we provide with 3D renderings before work begins.

Common Questions

Remodeling in Surprise — FAQs

Do you specialize in Sun City Grand aging-in-place remodeling?

Yes. Sun City Grand is fully within our West Valley service area and aging-in-place remodeling is one of our core specialties. The most common projects there are curbless shower conversions, grab bar installation (anchored to blocking, not drywall), comfort-height toilet replacement, and lever hardware throughout. We recommend planning these modifications proactively rather than after a fall or health event.

What's the most common upgrade in Marley Park homes?

Kitchen countertop and cabinet upgrades are the most common in Marley Park. The typical project: remove original laminate countertops, install quartz, replace cabinet doors and hardware, add under-cabinet LED lighting, and update the backsplash. For full kitchen remodels, clients often add a kitchen island or extend cabinet runs to the ceiling. Most projects run $18,000–$38,000.

Do you handle whole-house remodeling in Surprise?

Yes. For larger projects — full kitchen + primary bathroom + flooring throughout — we coordinate all trades under a single contract. This is more efficient than hiring separately for each trade and provides a single point of accountability. Whole-house projects in Surprise typically run $60,000–$150,000+ depending on the scope, home size, and finish level.
