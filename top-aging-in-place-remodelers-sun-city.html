Best for bathroom-first remodels that still look like home

We handle aging-in-place remodeling in stages. The bathroom comes first because it carries the most risk in the smallest space, then thresholds and lighting, then reach and storage in the kitchen. Typical bathroom work includes a curbless shower in place of the original tub, blocking behind the tile, comfort-height toilets, a bench and a handheld sprayer. How we built this list

We included only companies whose own websites say they serve Sun City or Sun City West, and we state where each one is actually based. Certifications are listed only where the company claims them on its own site. Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist (CAPS) is a National Association of Home Builders designation, and it is worth asking any contractor whether the person designing your project holds it. We left out star ratings on purpose.

What to do first

If you only do one thing, make it the shower. Replacing a step-over tub with a low-entry or curbless shower, a fold-down or built-in bench, a handheld sprayer and properly blocked grab bars removes the most common bathroom fall risk. After that, look at lighting on the route to the bathroom at night and at thresholds between rooms. Our aging-in-place bathroom guide covers each step, and our VA bathroom grant guide explains funding if you are a veteran.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best aging-in-place remodeler in Sun City, AZ?

For bathroom-first accessibility remodels designed to look like a normal home, we recommend Infinity Kitchens and Baths (AZ ROC #339999), which is our company. Renewed Life Properties is the pick based in Sun City itself, and Re-Bath Peoria says its team is certified in aging-in-place design.

What does aging-in-place remodeling usually include?

Most projects start in the bathroom: a curbless or low-entry shower, a bench, a handheld sprayer, grab bars on solid blocking and a comfort-height toilet. Later stages cover lighting, level thresholds, wider doorways and kitchen storage you can reach without bending or climbing.

Do I need HOA approval for an interior remodel in Sun City West?

Check with PORA (the Property Owners and Residents Association of Sun City West) before work starts. Interior remodels often still require notification or paperwork, and your contractor should know the process.
