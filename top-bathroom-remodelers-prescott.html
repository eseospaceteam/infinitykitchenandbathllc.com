Quick answer: Our six picks for bathroom remodeling in Prescott in 2026 are Infinity Kitchens and Baths (full bathroom remodels, walk-in and groutless showers), Integrated Kitchen & Bath (walk-in shower conversions), Ability Remodeling (luxury primary suites), York Custom Construction (owner-run custom tile), Re-Bath Prescott (fast system-built bath replacements) and Barrett Floors (tile-forward baths from a local flooring store).

Bathroom remodelers in Prescott fall into roughly three groups. Some are full-service contractors who move plumbing and rebuild the whole room. Some are system installers who replace a tub or shower with a manufactured surround in a few days. And some are tile and flooring specialists. None of these is automatically the better choice. What matters is matching the company to the job.

We are a bathroom remodeler ourselves, so we list ourselves first and say so at the top of the page. The five companies after us are real, independent Prescott-area businesses. For each one we note who they suit best. If you want the full vetting checklist, read how to choose a bathroom remodeler in Prescott.

How we built this list

System install or custom remodel?

This is the most useful question to answer before you call anyone. A system install replaces your tub or shower with a manufactured surround, usually quickly and at a predictable price, but with fewer design choices. A custom remodel can move plumbing, add a curbless shower or change the layout, and takes longer. Our bathroom remodel cost guide and our comparison of groutless and tile showers explain the trade-offs in Prescott terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best bathroom remodeler in Prescott, AZ?

It depends on the job. For full remodels, walk-in and groutless showers, we recommend Infinity Kitchens and Baths (AZ ROC #339999), which is our company. For a quick system-built replacement, Re-Bath Prescott is an option. For owner-run tile work, look at York Custom Construction. Verify any license at roc.az.gov.

Is a system shower or a tile shower better?

A system shower installs faster, has no grout lines to scrub, and gives you a more predictable price. Tile offers more design freedom and suits curbless and custom layouts. Groutless wall panels fall in between: a custom look without the grout maintenance.
