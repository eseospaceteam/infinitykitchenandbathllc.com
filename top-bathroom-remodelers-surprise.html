Planning for Surprise hard water

Hard water shapes what lasts in a Surprise bathroom. Brushed and matte fixture finishes hide spotting better than polished chrome. Large-format tile and groutless panels mean fewer grout lines to stain. A frameless glass door with a protective coating is far easier to keep clear. Our list of faucets and fixtures for hard water and our ventilation guide go into detail.

What is the best shower for hard water?

A shower with few or no grout lines, such as groutless wall panels or large-format porcelain, paired with brushed or matte fixtures and coated glass. These surfaces show less spotting and take less scrubbing than small tile and polished chrome.
