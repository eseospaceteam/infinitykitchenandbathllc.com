Best Cabinet Installers in Prescott (2026)

Disclosure: this list is published by Infinity Kitchens and Baths, and we ranked ourselves #1. Every other company is a real, independent local business we looked up in September 2026; none paid to be included, and we have no business relationship with any of them. Verify any contractor’s license at roc.az.gov before you hire.

By Steve Hunt, Owner & Lead Designer at Infinity Kitchens and Baths

Updated September 22, 2026 • 8 min read

Quick answer: The six cabinet companies we would call in Prescott in 2026 are Infinity Kitchens and Baths (factory-direct cabinets installed as part of a full remodel), Tim’s Cabinet Company (semi-custom brands, family trade since 1986), MCK Woodworks (shop-built custom cabinetry and appliances), Engrained Cabinetry & Countertops (cabinets and countertops from one dealer), Prescott Kitchens (design-led cabinetry) and Kepner’s Custom Cabinets (small-shop custom work in Chino Valley).

“Cabinet installer” covers very different businesses. Custom shops build boxes to order in their own workshop. Dealers sell and install semi-custom lines from national manufacturers. Remodelers install cabinets as one part of a larger kitchen project. The right choice depends on whether you are replacing cabinets only or remodeling the whole kitchen.

We install cabinets as part of full kitchen and bath remodels, which is why we list ourselves first. That is our bias, and we are stating it plainly. The other five are independent local cabinet businesses, each with a different strength. For the vetting checklist, see how to choose a cabinet maker in Prescott.

How we built this list

We included cabinet businesses with a real shop or showroom in the Prescott area and left out national online retailers. For each one we recorded only what its own website states. Where a company prints an Arizona ROC number, we show it. Whoever you hire, look the license up at roc.az.gov: cabinet installation in Arizona normally requires a licensed contractor.

Custom, semi-custom or stock?

Custom cabinets are built to your exact dimensions and take the longest. Semi-custom lines come in set sizes with many options for finish, depth and interior fittings. Stock cabinets ship quickly and cost the least. Our custom vs. RTA cabinets guide and cabinet cost guide cover the differences, and our lists of cabinet colors and hardware styles help with the design choices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best cabinet installer in Prescott, AZ?

If the cabinets are part of a full kitchen or bath remodel, we recommend Infinity Kitchens and Baths (AZ ROC #339999), which is our company. For semi-custom brands, Tim’s Cabinet Company is well established. For fully custom shop-built work, look at MCK Woodworks or Kepner’s Custom Cabinets.

Do cabinet installers need a license in Arizona?

Generally yes. Contracting work such as cabinet installation is regulated by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Ask for the ROC number and check it at roc.az.gov before you sign.

Should I hire a cabinet shop or a remodeler?

If you are replacing cabinets only, a cabinet shop or dealer is often simplest. If new cabinets mean new countertops, plumbing, electrical or flooring, a remodeler coordinates all of it under one contract and one warranty.
