Quick answer: The six kitchen and bath remodelers we would call in Prescott Valley in 2026 are Infinity Kitchens and Baths (kitchens and baths with factory-direct materials), Able & Ready Construction (design center in Prescott Valley since 2007), Crosby Home Services (interior plus decks), Re-Bath Prescott (fast bathroom replacements), Integrated Kitchen & Bath (published pricing) and Covenant Builders and Renovation (remodels combined with additions).

How we built this list

We included companies that either have an office in Prescott Valley or name it as a service area on their own website, and left out lead-generation sites that pass your details to unnamed contractors. Each entry uses only what the company publishes about itself. Star ratings are left out on purpose.

What Prescott Valley homeowners usually ask for

In our experience the most common requests are replacing a builder-grade kitchen (oak or thermofoil cabinets, laminate tops), converting a step-over tub to a walk-in shower, and making a bathroom easier to use for the long term. The Town of Prescott Valley issues its own permits, and whichever contractor you hire should pull them. Our Yavapai County permit guide covers the details.
