How we built this list

We included companies based in Peoria or Glendale, or with a page on their own website saying they serve those cities, and left out referral networks and lead-generation sites. Every fact comes from the company’s own site, and we left out star ratings on purpose. Check any ROC number at roc.az.gov before you sign.

Peoria or Glendale: who issues the permit?

Each city runs its own building department, and addresses near the city line are not always in the city you would expect. Your contractor should confirm which jurisdiction you are in and pull the permit for any work that touches plumbing, electrical or structure. Our Peoria kitchen page and Glendale kitchen page cover local permitting in more detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best kitchen remodeler in Peoria or Glendale, AZ?

For full kitchen remodels with factory-direct cabinets and stone, we recommend Infinity Kitchens and Baths (AZ ROC #339999), which is our company. For wall removal and open layouts, Crafted Remodeling in Glendale is a strong option, and Kitchen Tune-Up Northwest Valley fits cabinet refacing.

Does Infinity Kitchens and Baths serve Peoria and Glendale?

Yes. We are based in Prescott and remodel kitchens and bathrooms throughout Peoria, Glendale and eight other West Valley cities. Our West Valley line is 602-885-6998.

Why is Infinity ranked first?

Infinity Kitchens and Baths publishes this list, and we disclose that at the top. The other companies are real, independent businesses that serve Peoria and Glendale. None of them paid to be included.

Is refacing cheaper than a kitchen remodel?

Usually, because refacing keeps the existing cabinet boxes and layout. It only makes sense when the boxes are solid and the layout works. If you want to move the sink, add an island or open a wall, you need a full remodel.
