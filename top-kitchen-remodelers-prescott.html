Updated September 22, 2026 • 8 min read

Quick answer: The six kitchen remodelers we would point a Prescott homeowner to in 2026 are Infinity Kitchens and Baths (full kitchen remodels with factory-direct cabinets and countertops), Ability Remodeling (luxury design-build), Integrated Kitchen & Bath (published pricing), Prescott Kitchens (design-led cabinetry), Able & Ready Construction (Prescott Valley design center) and Crosby Home Services (kitchens plus decks). Prescott has plenty of people who will remodel a kitchen, and most of them look alike on a search results page. What actually separates them is the kind of kitchen they are set up to build: some are luxury design-build firms, some are cabinet designers, some are flooring stores that also do remodels, and some are general contractors who do a bit of everything.

This list is our honest read of the local field. We put ourselves first because this is our site and a full kitchen remodel is exactly what we do. Everyone else on the list is a real, independent company with a working local office, and for each one we note who they suit best, so you can call the two or three that match your project instead of all six. How we built this list

We started with every kitchen remodeler we could find with a real office in the Prescott area, then dropped lead-generation sites that pass your details to unnamed "partners," companies based outside Yavapai County, and any site that looked abandoned or unfinished. For the companies left, we read their websites and wrote down only what they say about themselves: where they are, what they build, and the license number they publish. We did not use star ratings, because they change weekly and are easy to game.

The order after #1 is our judgment of how complete a kitchen service each company offers. It is not a score. The right choice for you depends on the project, which is why every entry has a "best for" line.

Before you call anyone: three checks

  • License. Look the number up at roc.az.gov. It should be active, in a classification that covers the work, and free of unresolved complaints. Our licensing and insurance page walks through how.
  • Who does the work. Ask whether the people who design the kitchen also build it, or whether it goes to subcontractors.
  • What the quote covers. Compare scope line by line: demolition, electrical, plumbing, drywall repair, and who pulls the permit. Our kitchen remodel cost guide shows what a complete Prescott quote should include.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the best kitchen remodeler in Prescott, AZ?

It depends on the project. For a full kitchen remodel with factory-direct cabinets and countertops, we recommend Infinity Kitchens and Baths (AZ ROC #339999), which is our company. For luxury design-build, Ability Remodeling is a strong option. For design-led cabinetry, look at Prescott Kitchens. Whoever you choose, verify the license at roc.az.gov.

How do I check a kitchen remodeler’s license in Arizona?

Go to roc.az.gov, open the license lookup, and search by license number or company name. Confirm the license is active, that its classification covers the work, and that there are no unresolved complaints. It takes about a minute and is free.
