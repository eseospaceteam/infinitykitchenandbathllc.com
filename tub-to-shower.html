Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Prescott, AZ

Replace an outdated, unused tub with a spacious, modern walk-in shower. Fast installation, groutless options, and lifetime warranty coverage.

Tub Removal & Shower Installation

Transform That Unused Tub Into Something You'll Love

If you're like most homeowners, your bathtub sits unused while your shower gets all the attention. Converting that tub space into a walk-in shower gives you more room, more functionality, and a bathroom that works better for your actual lifestyle.

Infinity Kitchen and Bath handles every aspect of the conversion — from removing the existing tub and surround to installing a fully custom shower with your choice of walls, floor, fixtures, and enclosure. We offer groutless solid-surface wall panels for a low-maintenance finish, as well as tile options for a more traditional look.

  • Complete tub removal and disposal
  • Custom shower pan, walls, and enclosure
  • Groutless solid-surface or tile wall options
  • Glass doors, frameless enclosures, or walk-in openings
  • Optional bench seating, niche shelving, and grab bars
  • Lifetime warranty on groutless systems

Recommended Upgrade

Add a Groutless System to Your Conversion

When converting your tub, we highly recommend pairing it with our groutless solid-surface shower system. You'll get a seamless, beautiful finish with zero grout to ever clean or re-seal — backed by a full lifetime warranty from both Infinity Kitchen and Bath and the manufacturer.
