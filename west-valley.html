West Valley — Phoenix Metro

West Valley Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling

Eight cities across the West Valley, from Buckeye to Sun City West — with free in-home consultations and a licensed Arizona crew. Call 602-885-6998.

We remodel kitchens and bathrooms across eight West Valley cities: Avondale, Buckeye, Glendale, Goodyear, Peoria, Surprise, Sun City and Sun City West. Our West Valley line is 602-885-6998.

The free in-home consultation works exactly as it does in Prescott — we come to you with design tools and samples and leave a detailed, no-obligation quote. Pick your city below to go straight to kitchens, bathrooms or full services.

Infinity Kitchens and Baths has remodeled kitchens and bathrooms since 2011, based in Prescott and working throughout Yavapai County and the West Valley of the Phoenix metro. Two things brought us down into the metro and keep us there.

The first is the housing stock. A great deal of the West Valley went up between the mid-1990s and the late 2000s, which means an enormous number of homes are now hitting the age where the original builder-grade kitchen and the original bathrooms are simply worn out — laminate tops, oak cabinets, cultured-marble vanities, and a step-over tub-shower combo in the primary bath.

The second is the active-adult communities. Sun City and Sun City West in particular are full of original Del Webb bathrooms that were never designed for someone who now has trouble stepping over a tub wall. Converting those safely, without making a bathroom look institutional, is some of the most rewarding work we do.

What We Do Here

The West Valley Projects We See Most

Builder-grade kitchen updates. Homes from the late-1990s and 2000s boom typically shipped with oak or maple cabinets, laminate or entry-level granite tops, and a cramped island. Replacing tops and either refinishing or replacing the cabinets transforms the room without moving a single wall. Start at kitchen remodeling or price it with the cost calculator.

Tub-to-shower conversions. Far and away our most requested West Valley bathroom project, and the one with the biggest day-to-day payoff. See tub-to-shower conversion and the cost breakdown, or the full shower guide if you are weighing shower types.

Aging-in-place bathrooms. Curbless entries, grab-bar blocking, comfort-height fixtures and slip-resistant floors — built into a bathroom that still looks like a bathroom. See aging in place, ADA bathrooms and curbless showers.

Hard-water-proof showers. Metro water is hard, and grout is where that shows first. Groutless solid-surface systems remove the problem instead of managing it — compare them on groutless vs tile.

Countertop replacement on its own. Often the single highest-impact change in a builder-grade kitchen. See the countertops hub for materials and installed costs.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you actually work in the West Valley, or only Prescott?

Both. Our showroom and office are in Prescott and we have served Yavapai County since 2011, and we run projects throughout the West Valley of the Phoenix metro. Our West Valley line is 602-885-6998 — call that number rather than the Prescott one and you will reach the right side of the schedule straight away.

Which West Valley cities do you cover?

Avondale, Buckeye, Glendale, Goodyear, Peoria, Surprise, Sun City and Sun City West. If your address is nearby but not on that list, call 602-885-6998 and we will tell you plainly whether we can serve it well — we would rather say no than stretch a crew too thin.

Is the consultation still free if I am in the Phoenix metro?

Yes. The free in-home consultation works the same way in the West Valley as it does in Prescott: we come to you with design tools and material samples, measure the space, talk through what you want, and leave you with a detailed no-obligation quote. Coming to you is how we do it everywhere — you should not have to drive to a showroom to get a price.

Do you do a lot of aging-in-place work in Sun City and Sun City West?

Yes — it is one of the main reasons we work in the West Valley at all. The active-adult communities there are full of original Del Webb bathrooms with step-over tubs in the only full bath. Converting those to a walk-in shower or a curbless entry, with proper blocking for grab bars, is work we do constantly. See our aging-in-place and ADA bathroom pages.

Are you licensed to work in Maricopa County?

Yes. Infinity Kitchens and Baths is a licensed Arizona contractor — AZ ROC #339999, bonded and insured — and an Arizona ROC licence is statewide, not county-by-county. You can verify it anytime at roc.az.gov. Permits are pulled through the relevant city or Maricopa County depending on the jurisdiction and scope.

Does being based in Prescott affect the schedule or the price?

It affects how we schedule, not what you pay. We batch West Valley projects so crews are working full days in the metro rather than commuting back and forth, which is also why our West Valley calendar has defined windows rather than same-week starts. Your quote is a fixed price with no travel surcharge buried in it.
