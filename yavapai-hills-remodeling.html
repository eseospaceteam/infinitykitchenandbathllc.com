Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling in Yavapai Hills, AZ

Yavapai Hills was first developed in the 1970s and has been growing ever since, so the community spans a wide range of home ages — from 1970s and 80s ranch homes ready for full modernization to 2000s-and-newer custom homes that just need a refresh. What Yavapai Hills homes share is space: larger lots, bigger rooms, and floor plans that give us room to work.

That space opens up the kind of projects a lot of Prescott neighborhoods can’t support — generous islands with seating, opening the kitchen to a great room, walk-in pantries, dual-vanity spa master baths, and curbless showers. For the community’s many long-time residents, we also do a lot of tasteful aging-in-place work that keeps a home comfortable and safe for the long run without looking clinical.

As a family-owned, factory-direct remodeler, we bring showroom-quality cabinetry, stone, and tile to Yavapai Hills homes without the markup — with one accountable team from design through completion.

Remodeling Yavapai Hills’ Custom Homes

Because Yavapai Hills homes are larger and custom, the remodels here tend to be more ambitious: full kitchen renovations with big islands, custom cabinetry, and quartz or quartzite counters; opening walls between the kitchen and living areas; and master baths reimagined with curbless walk-in showers, freestanding tubs, and double vanities.

A couple of local notes: Yavapai Hills is an HOA community with architectural guidelines for exterior-visible work (interior remodels are typically unaffected), and its hillside, larger-lot homes sometimes have longer plumbing runs and well or shared-water considerations we account for in planning. We handle the City of Prescott / Yavapai County permits and inspections your project requires.

FAQ

Yavapai Hills Remodeling — Common Questions

Do you remodel homes in Yavapai Hills?

Yes — Yavapai Hills is a core service area. We remodel kitchens and bathrooms throughout the community, from 1970s–80s ranch homes needing full modernization to newer custom homes wanting a refresh.

Can you handle large, custom kitchen projects?

Absolutely — and Yavapai Hills homes are ideal for them. The larger floor plans here give us room for generous islands with seating, walk-in pantries, opened-up layouts to great rooms, and custom cabinetry. We design and build the whole project in-house.

Do you do aging-in-place remodels in Yavapai Hills?

Yes. Many Yavapai Hills residents have lived here for years, and we do a lot of tasteful aging-in-place work — curbless showers, grab bars integrated into the tile, comfort-height vanities, and slip-resistant flooring — that keeps a home safe and comfortable without looking institutional.

How much does a remodel cost in Yavapai Hills?

Because Yavapai Hills homes and projects tend to be larger, kitchens commonly run $30,000–$70,000+ and master baths $20,000–$45,000, depending on size and finishes. We provide a detailed written estimate after a free in-home consultation.
